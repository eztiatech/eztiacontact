HydraVolt™ Technology

Cooling built into every thread.

HydraVolt™ is a proprietary cooling gel that bonds directly to performance fabric. When body temperature rises, it activates, drawing heat away from the skin and dispersing it through the fabric's microstructure. The result: meaningfully lower skin surface temperature, improving performance and recovery.
