HydraVolt™ Technology

Cooling built into every thread.

HydraVolt™ is a proprietary phase-change cooling gel that bonds directly to performance fabric. When core temperature rises, it activates — drawing heat away from the skin and dispersing it through the fabric's microstructure. The result is up to 4°C lower skin surface temperature. Clearer focus. Fewer incidents.

Activates at body temperature

No triggers, no switches. HydraVolt™ responds to your core — automatically.

No refrigeration or charging needed

Self-contained technology. Nothing to charge, store, or prepare before your shift.

Washable and durable for site use

Engineered to survive the conditions you work in — wash after wash, shift after shift.
